DETAIL A · SCALE 1:1

Mechanical Engineer interested in machine learning, finite element modeling, and neural networks.

I work at the intersection of computational mechanics and data. I use FEM for material behavior, structural mechanics, transient response, heat transfer, and thermo-mechanical analysis, and I apply neural networks and physics-informed neural networks (PINNs) to constitutive modeling, parameter identification, surrogate modeling, and remaining-life prediction.

Today I research tire rubber aging, durability, and fatigue at The University of Akron. Before that, I spent three years at Kathmandu University's Energy Systems and Technology Research Laboratory on EV powertrains, battery health prognostics, and thermal management, and I've built and converted real vehicles along the way.
