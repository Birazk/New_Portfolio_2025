Eicher Skyline Bus: Diesel to Electric Conversion

Converting a popular diesel city bus, the Eicher 10.75H Skyline, into a zero-emissions electric bus, with a documented process for large-scale retrofits in Nepal.

Project goal

With government support for retrofitting commercial vehicles, the project set out to convert a diesel city bus to a battery-electric drivetrain and to build a repeatable, safety-conscious process for doing so, covering powertrain architecture, structural integrity, and digital modeling.

My role

I worked on the engineering team under Prof. Bivek Baral and led system integration and simulation.

  • Powertrain modeling: built MATLAB/Simulink models for component selection and performance evaluation on real-world driving cycles; the analysis projects a ~60% reduction in energy cost per kilometer.
  • 3D scanning and digital twin: helped scan the bus to build a precise digital model as the base for design and analysis.
  • CAD: modeled the C-channel ladder-frame chassis and new EV components (motor mounts, battery packs) in SolidWorks 2022 R1.
  • FEA: analyzed the chassis in ANSYS at gross vehicle weight and a 25% overload.
  • Disassembly: took part in the safe removal of the engine, transmission, fuel, and exhaust systems.
Digital design and chassis validation

The CAD model of the ladder frame served as the blueprint for placing the motor, battery packs, and controller with balanced weight distribution. FEA confirmed the frame safely carries the gross vehicle weight and even a 25% overload, with a maximum deformation of 6.85 mm.

Physical disassembly

Following strict safety procedures, the team removed the engine, fuel tank, and exhaust over 4 days, preparing the chassis for the electric drive system.

Status and next steps
  • Chassis design validated by FEA for the electric conversion.
  • Digital-twin foundation created from 3D scanning.
  • Documented, safety-first workflow for heavy-vehicle powertrain removal.
  • Next phases: motor integration, battery enclosures, high-voltage wiring, and on-road testing.
