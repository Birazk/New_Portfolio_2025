Capabilities
  • Kinematics: analytical forward and inverse kinematics with singularity detection.
  • Dynamics: full Lagrangian model with mass, Coriolis, and gravity terms.
  • Control: PID, computed torque, adaptive, and robust sliding-mode controllers.
  • Trajectories: polynomial point-to-point, circular, and figure-8 paths.
  • Analysis: energy efficiency, tracking accuracy, and stability metrics, with data export.
