Maruti 800: IC Engine to Electric Vehicle Conversion

Turning a 28-year-old gasoline car into a fully electric vehicle through custom design, fabrication, and systems integration.

Objective

The project bridged theory and practice: a complete mechanical and electrical conversion of a conventional car, tackling powertrain design, weight distribution, battery management, and high-voltage safety.

My role
  • CAD: designed motor and battery-box mounts in SolidWorks for strength and balanced weight.
  • Component selection: chose the AC induction motor, controller, and Li-ion packs with an integrated BMS.
  • Fabrication: removed the engine, exhaust, and fuel system; welded, fitted, and installed the new drivetrain.
  • Electrical integration: built the high- and low-voltage harnesses for the batteries, controller, motor, charger, and auxiliaries.
  • Testing: ran safety checks and road tests for performance, range, and thermal behavior.
Powertrain mounting

An adapter plate and motor mount couple the electric motor to the car's original manual gearbox, reusing the original engine mounting points to minimize chassis changes.

Battery system

Battery boxes were fabricated and placed front and rear to approximate the original weight distribution, and wired to the BMS for cell monitoring and protection.

High-voltage integration

The final stage wired the motor controller, DC-DC converter, on-board charger, and contactors, with every connection routed, insulated, and tested before the first drive.

Outcome
  • A working zero-emissions car capable of daily driving.
  • Practical skills in CAD, welding and fitting, HV design, and project management.
  • A full-system understanding of EV architecture, and data that fed the published energy-consumption study.
