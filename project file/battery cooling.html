The challenge

Lithium-ion cells age faster when hot, and unevenly when some cells run hotter than others. Under high loads a poor cooling plate leaves hot spots that shorten the whole pack's life and raise safety risk.

Approach
  • CFD model: built a conjugate heat-transfer model of the module and cooling plate in ANSYS Fluent.
  • Validation: matched experimental temperature data within 5%.
  • Design study: compared five channel layouts, from standard serpentine to parallel mini-channels.
  • Optimization: developed a variable-width serpentine channel that speeds up coolant over known hot spots.
Results
  • Peak temperature gradients across the pack reduced by more than 5 °C.
  • Temperature uniformity improved by about 15%.
  • Projected battery cycle-life increase of up to 20% under high load.
