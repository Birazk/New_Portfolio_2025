Tire rubber aging, fatigue & physics-informed modeling

My research at The University of Akron links how tire rubber ages in service (ozone, heat, and humidity) to how it stiffens, dissipates energy, cracks, and wears. The goal is a predictive framework that ties real aging conditions to how the compound behaves and fails.

unagedaged

Why tire aging matters

Stiffness and hysteresis shift

Oxidative aging changes the rubber's stress–strain response and energy dissipation over time.

Shorter fatigue life

Aged compound cracks and fails sooner under the same cyclic loading than fresh material.

A missing link in models

Most durability models still assume unaged properties; few connect environmental exposure to constitutive behavior and failure.

Methods & tools

Dynamic characterization

Direct dynamic characterization of tire elastomers from 20 to 1500 Hz, controlling temperature, prestrain, strain amplitude, and loading history.

Physics-informed neural networks

PINNs that discover hyperelastic constitutive models from sparse experimental data by embedding mechanical constraints directly in the loss function, then learn how the constitutive law evolves with aging from combined test and simulation data.

Next steps

  • Export oxygen profiles over time and integrate the reaction rate into accumulated oxygen exposure.
  • Calibrate property evolution with aging-dependent tests at 7-day, 2-week, and 4-week intervals.
  • Map aged properties into Abaqus mechanics and predict fatigue life with Endurica under pressure-cycle loading.
  • Train PINNs on combined experimental and simulation data to predict tire-rubber performance.
