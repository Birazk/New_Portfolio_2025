Sketch 1 · EV energy · from my 2024 paper

On hilly roads, altitude, not speed, drains the battery

When we logged GPS and BMS data on our converted EV over a hilly route, altitude and state of charge had a correlation of −0.89. Climbing was the single biggest driver of battery drain, which is why lab range figures from flat cycles mislead drivers in places like Nepal.

The other half of the story is regeneration: the route's energy loss fell from 20.86 kWh without regenerative braking to 10.22 kWh with it. On terrain like this, regen is essential, not an add-on.

Sketch 2 · Thermal management · CFD

Put the coolant where the heat is: variable-width channels

I compared five cooling-plate layouts for a lithium-ion module in ANSYS Fluent, from a standard serpentine to parallel mini-channels. The best design wasn't the most complex one: it was a serpentine whose width changes so coolant speeds up over the known hot spots.

That targeted change cut peak temperature gradients by more than 5 °C and improved uniformity by about 15%, which projects to as much as 20% more cycle life under high load.

Sketch 3 · Battery health · machine learning

Fleet data makes battery health predictable

Using real-time data from five public electric buses, our ML models forecast battery state of health with reported accuracy above 95%. That's enough to plan maintenance before a pack becomes a problem, and to decide which packs are good candidates for a second life.

I later turned the workflow into an open-source Streamlit app with ingestion, training, real-time prediction, and a fleet dashboard.

Sketch 4 · Tire research · in progress

Most durability models assume the rubber never ages

Tires age in service long before they wear out: ozone, heat, and humidity change how the compound stiffens and dissipates energy, and aged rubber cracks sooner under the same loads. Yet most durability models still use unaged material properties.

My current research connects aging experiments, Abaqus and Endurica simulation, and physics-informed neural networks to close that gap.
