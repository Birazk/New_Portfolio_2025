Who I Am

Bridging Engineering Physics with Data Intelligence

I am a Mechanical Engineer with a strong interest in machine learning, finite element modeling, and neural networks. I use FEM for material behavior, structural mechanics, transient response, heat transfer, and thermo-mechanical analysis, and I apply neural networks and physics-informed neural networks (PINNs) to constitutive modeling, parameter identification, surrogate modeling, and remaining-life prediction. My experience spans tire aging and durability, electric-vehicle powertrains, battery state-of-health prognostics, thermal management, and experimental, data-driven engineering analysis.

My Expertise

I combine mechanical engineering fundamentals with simulation, machine learning, and experimental data to solve complex problems in materials, mobility, and energy systems.


Rubber Aging, Durability & Fatigue

I characterize the dynamic behavior of tire elastomers (20–1500 Hz) and build Abaqus workflows that simulate thermal, humidity, oxygen, and ozone aging, feeding Endurica-based fatigue and durability analysis.


Physics-Informed Machine Learning

I am developing physics-informed neural network approaches to discover hyperelastic constitutive models from sparse experimental data by embedding mechanical constraints directly into neural loss functions.


ABAQUS, FEM & Tire Wear Modeling

I use Abaqus-based finite element simulations with machine learning and experimental aging data to model performance degradation and predict tire wear rates under different environmental and operational conditions.


EV Powertrain Modeling & System Integration

I have worked on electric vehicle conversion, public bus electrification, drivetrain component selection, and MATLAB/Simulink-based powertrain modeling to validate performance against real-world driving cycles.


Battery Health Prognostics & Thermal Management

My work includes battery State-of-Health prediction using machine learning, real-time BMS data, CAN bus/OBD-II data acquisition, and CFD-based battery cooling design for improved cycle life and safety.

What Drives Me

I am motivated by research that turns engineering models into practical, real-world systems. Whether working on tire wear prediction, electric mobility, battery health, or sustainable energy, I aim to build solutions that are technically rigorous, data-driven, and useful for society.

Research & Projects

Research Highlights

Selected work across machine learning, finite element simulation, EV systems, battery prognostics, CFD thermal management, and sustainable energy technologies.

Tire Elastomer Dynamics & Aging

Dynamic characterization of tire elastomers with controlled temperature, prestrain, and strain amplitude, plus Abaqus aging simulations and Endurica-based fatigue analysis.

Physics-Informed Neural Networks for Materials Modeling

Developing PINN-based methods to discover hyperelastic constitutive models from sparse experimental data using embedded mechanical constraints.

Tire Wear Prediction Using ML and Abaqus FEM

Integrating experimental aging data, machine learning, and Abaqus simulations to predict tire wear and performance degradation under varied conditions.

Electric Public Bus Conversion

Led system integration and MATLAB/Simulink modeling for converting an ICE public bus into a battery-electric drivetrain with a projected 60% reduction in energy cost per kilometer.

Battery SOH Prognostics

Developed machine learning models with over 95% accuracy to forecast battery degradation using real-time data from a fleet of five public electric buses.

Battery Liquid Cooling CFD Simulation

Built ANSYS Fluent CFD models for battery thermal management, achieving less than 5% validation error and improving pack temperature uniformity.

IC Engine to EV Retrofitting

Converted a 28-year-old Maruti 800 into a fully electric vehicle, including drivetrain, battery pack, controls, testing, and optimization.
